How EllaPass checks halal status

We only show what we can verify

A place only carries a halal badge after the owner visits in person, the business confirms their halal level in writing, and we record the date. Until then, places show as "Not yet verified". We never add a halal label based on reviews alone.

The certifying body for halal food in Sri Lanka is the Halal Assessment Council (HAC). A "certified" label means the place holds a current HAC certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Is it easy to find halal food in Ella?

Ella has a small Muslim community and a few restaurants that serve halal food. It is not as easy as in a larger city, but with the guide on this page you can find suitable options. Vegetarian dishes are widely available and a safe option at any restaurant.

Are most Sri Lankan restaurants halal?

No. Many Sri Lankan restaurants serve pork and alcohol alongside other dishes. Always confirm halal status before ordering. "No pork" does not automatically mean halal meat sourcing.

Where is the nearest mosque?

The nearest mosques are in Bandarawela (about 20 km) and Badulla (about 15 km). Jumu'ah times vary — confirm with the mosque directly. We are working on adding verified mosque information to this page. Check before you go

Can I request halal meals at a guesthouse?

Some guesthouses will prepare halal meals on request if you give them notice in advance. Use the halal meals tick on the EllaPass booking form and we will match you with a guesthouse that can accommodate this.

How does EllaPass verify halal status?

We only add a halal badge after a physical visit to the restaurant, written confirmation from the owner of their halal level, and a record of the date we checked. We re-check every three months or when a reader reports a change. A place that carries no badge has not been verified yet.
