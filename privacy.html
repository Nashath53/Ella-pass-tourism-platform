Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send a booking request or feedback, we receive: your name, WhatsApp number, optional email address, service type, date, destination and any notes you write.

We do not collect payment details. No payment is taken through this website.

We do not use cookies. Your theme choice and saved list are stored only in your own browser and never sent to us.

Who sees your data

For a booking: your name and WhatsApp number are shared with the driver or host you are matched with. No one else.

For feedback: your message is read by the EllaPass team only. If you ticked "You may show my comment", an approved version (first name and country only) may appear on the site.

We use a third-party form service (Web3Forms) to receive messages. They receive the same information and hold it according to their own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Message us on WhatsApp with the booking code or feedback date and we will delete it within 7 days.

Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act applies to personal data controllers. EllaPass processes personal data and will comply with the Act's requirements as they come into full effect.
