About EllaPass

A free, locally made guide to Ella, Sri Lanka — for visitors and for people who live here.

Who runs it

A local project

EllaPass is run by a local team in Ella. We are not a travel agency. We connect visitors with independent drivers and hosts, and we provide a free guide to help people find their way around the area.

Service area

40 km from Ella town

EllaPass covers places, drivers and guides within about 40 km road distance from Ella town. This includes Nine Arch Bridge, Ravana Falls, Demodara, Lipton's Seat, Bandarawela and Badulla.

How listings work

Verified means checked

A "Verified" label means we visited the place in person and confirmed the details. "Check before you go" means the information is sourced but changes often. "Not yet verified" means we have one weak source only. We never invent ratings or reviews.

How we earn money

Small, honest fees

EllaPass earns a small fixed fee per completed booking made through the site. "Sponsored" listings are paid — they are always clearly labelled. Sponsored listings never get better ratings because they pay. The guide is free for visitors.

EllaPass connects visitors with independent drivers and hosts. We are not a licensed travel agent. Prices are agreed directly between the visitor and the driver or host. EllaPass is not responsible for arrangements made outside the booking form.
