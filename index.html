Good to know

Is EllaPass free to use?

Yes. The public guide is free for everyone. Businesses can pay to be featured, and paid listings are always labelled "Sponsored".

Are prices and train times exact?

No. They are guides. Prices are typical ranges and train times are approximate. Always confirm locally, and agree tuk-tuk prices before you go.

How do I book a room or a driver?

Tap WhatsApp on any card. A ready message opens in WhatsApp, and you agree details directly with the owner or driver. Nothing is paid on this website.

What is Guest Mode?

A private guide for guests of partner guesthouses, opened with an access code from your host. After checkout the private guide closes, and the public guide stays open.
